I presented our work on physics-informed machine learning for radar precipitation nowcasting at a University of Exeter meeting with the Met Office.

The most valuable part was the discussion around collaboration: how research metrics translate to forecasting practice, where physical constraints help, and which failure modes matter when a model moves beyond an experiment.

The session reinforced a principle I keep returning to: a better model is only the beginning. Useful research also needs credible evaluation, domain feedback and a path into the systems where decisions are made.
