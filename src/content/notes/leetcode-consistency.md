Crossing 500 solved LeetCode problems and maintaining a 200-day coding streak mattered less as a number than as evidence of a repeatable practice.

Daily problem solving sharpened pattern recognition, but the larger benefit was learning to stay with ambiguity: reduce a problem, test assumptions, inspect failures and improve the explanation—not just the accepted solution.

That habit carries into AI engineering. Models and frameworks change quickly; the ability to decompose unfamiliar systems and reason about tradeoffs lasts longer.
